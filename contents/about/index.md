# The Sensing the Ocean with Marine Radar (SOMaR) Community

The Sensing the Ocean with Marine Radar (SOMaR) community is an international network of scientists and engineers who use marine radar to observe the ocean.
Since the inaugural workshop in Lerici, Italy, in 2011, the community has met biennially to share research developments on the oceanographic use of marine radar technologies, most recently at the seventh workshop (SOMaR-7), held at Oregon State University in Corvallis, Oregon, on June 24-26, 2025.
The informal workshops provide a safe space to present recent results and discuss topics ranging from waves, wind, currents, and bathymetry to coherent radar systems, radar scattering mechanisms, data processing techniques, experimental developments, and comparisons with SAR and other imaging sensors.
More information is available on the [SOMaR-7 workshop page](https://blogs.oregonstate.edu/somar7/) and the [SOMaR GitHub organization](https://github.com/sensing-the-ocean-with-marine-radar).
