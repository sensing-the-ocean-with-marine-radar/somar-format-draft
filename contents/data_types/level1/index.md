# Level 1 data

Level 1 products contain the radar measurement itself, in the analog-to-digital converter units of the instrument and without geophysical interpretation.
They differ only in geometry: [Level 1a](level1a.md) preserves the raw pulse sequence in polar sensor coordinates, while [Level 1b](level1b.md) organizes individual antenna revolutions as images, either in polar coordinates with a regularized azimuth axis or on a Cartesian grid centered on the platform.
Level 1 files serve as the input for the Level 2 retrievals.
