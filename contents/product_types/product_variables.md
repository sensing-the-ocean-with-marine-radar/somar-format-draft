# Product variables

This page defines the data, ancillary, and coordinate variables of the [SOMaR products](index.md), apart from the coordinates, navigation, and quality flag that all products of a category share, which the page of the category defines.
Each variable keeps its meaning in every product that carries it; which products require, recommend, or allow it is stated with the products (see [Categories](index.md#categories)).
The convention a variable is taken from is named in small print under it (see [Metadata](../metadata_attributes/index.md#reading-the-tables)); a variable marked CF carries the CF standard name of the same name, unless its description says otherwise.

In the column of values, `(…)` stands for the dimensions that the products of a category share in their form: for polar products, `(time, range)` in the sweeps of [Level 1a](polar.md) and of [Level 1b pulse-averaged pointing data](polar.md#averaged-sweeps) and `(time, azimuth, range)` in the image cube of [Level 1b polar](polar.md#image-cube); for [gridded products](gridded.md), the dimensions of the horizontal grid, such as `(latitude, longitude)` or `(y, x)`, preceded by `time` from a fixed platform and in the image cube of [Level 1b Cartesian](gridded.md#image-cube-with-moving-origin); for [spatially averaged products](spatially_averaged.md), `(time)`.
Dimensions written beside it, such as `(…, wave_frequency)`, are added in the position shown.

On a [rotated local grid](../georeferencing/index.md#rotated-local-grids), the components of vectors are given along its axes: a variable described as taking the place of another on a rotated grid is carried instead of it, with its tier and with the variables it requires and is carried together with replaced in the same way, and the other is omitted.

Some variables are only meaningful together with others.
Their descriptions state this in one of two forms, which apply in every product: "Carried together with `x`." means that a file that carries the variable also carries `x`, and vice versa; "Requires `x`." means that a file that carries the variable also carries `x`, in the same group or in one of its ancestors.

| Variable | Values | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `background_noise`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Spectral density of the background noise in the radar images, used by the signal-to-noise ratio method to derive the significant wave height. Carried together with `wave_signal`. |
| `backscatter_amplitude`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | `(…)`; `1` or `dB` | Backscatter amplitude, e.g. the square root of (I^2 + Q^2) or the video signal of the radar, in uncalibrated analog-to-digital converter units. The name is the same at every level, so that it identifies the quantity; the FM 301 field names (Table 301-9) are not used, since they denote calibrated moments of weather radars. The values are stored in the units of the analog-to-digital converters, using [`scale_factor`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#scale_factor), [`add_offset`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#add_offset), and [`_FillValue`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#_FillValue); [`units`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#units) is `"1"` or `"dB"` for linear or logarithmic dimensionless units, and [`comment`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#comment) should describe how the values were derived from the radar signal. A file holds either this variable or `backscatter_power`, not both, since power is the square of amplitude. |
| `backscatter_power`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | `(…)`; `1` or `dB` | Backscatter power, e.g. (I^2 + Q^2), in uncalibrated analog-to-digital converter units, stored as `backscatter_amplitude`. A file holds either this variable or `backscatter_amplitude`, not both. |
| `correlation_coefficient`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Peak Pearson correlation coefficient of the forward cross-correlation from which the sea ice drift is retrieved. |
| `correlation_coefficient_backward`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Peak Pearson correlation coefficient of the backward cross-correlation. Requires `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward` and `northward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`. |
| `eastward_sea_ice_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Eastward component of the sea ice drift, from the forward retrieval, i.e. from the earlier to the later of two radar images. As the CF standard name implies, the drift is given relative to the ground, with the platform motion removed, and points in the direction toward which the ice drifts. Carried together with `northward_sea_ice_velocity`. |
| `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Eastward component of the sea ice drift, from the backward retrieval, i.e. from the later to the earlier of the two radar images, as an independent estimate of the same drift. Carries the standard name `eastward_sea_ice_velocity`. Carried together with `northward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`. |
| `eastward_sea_water_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Eastward component of the near-surface current. As the CF standard name implies, the current is given relative to the ground, with the platform motion removed, and points in the direction toward which the water flows. It is retrieved from the current-induced Doppler shift of the surface waves and is therefore a depth-weighted average, with the surface current carrying the greatest weight (Stewart and Joy [1974](../references/index.md#stewart-joy-1974)); its effective depth is indicated by `mean_wavenumber`. Retrieved within analysis windows that move with the platform, it is given relative to the ground, i.e. after adding the mean platform velocity to the current retrieved in the moving frame. Carried together with `northward_sea_water_velocity`. |
| `eastward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Standard error of the eastward component of the near-surface current, e.g. from a least-squares fit. Requires `eastward_sea_water_velocity`. Carried together with `northward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error`. |
| `eastward_wave_wavenumber`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(eastward_wave_wavenumber)`; `m-1` | Coordinate variable: the eastward wavenumber component of a two-dimensional wavenumber spectrum. Wavenumbers are cyclic, i.e. the reciprocal of the wavelength. By SOMaR convention, consistent with the from-direction of the frequency-direction spectra, the wavenumber vector points toward the direction from which the waves come, i.e. opposite to the direction of propagation. |
| `mean_wavenumber`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m-1` | Mean wavenumber of the wave signal used in the retrieval, cyclic, i.e. the reciprocal of the wavelength. It indicates the effective sensing depth of a current retrieval, approximately 4–8% of the corresponding wavelength, and, for a bathymetry retrieval, the greatest depth that can be retrieved, approximately 30% of that wavelength. |
| `northward_sea_ice_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Northward component of the sea ice drift, from the forward retrieval, defined as `eastward_sea_ice_velocity`. Carried together with `eastward_sea_ice_velocity`. |
| `northward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Northward component of the sea ice drift, from the backward retrieval, defined as `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`. Carries the standard name `northward_sea_ice_velocity`. Carried together with `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`. |
| `northward_sea_water_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Northward component of the near-surface current, defined as `eastward_sea_water_velocity`. Carried together with `eastward_sea_water_velocity`. |
| `northward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Standard error of the northward component of the near-surface current, e.g. from a least-squares fit. Requires `northward_sea_water_velocity`. Carried together with `eastward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error`. |
| `northward_wave_wavenumber`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(northward_wave_wavenumber)`; `m-1` | Coordinate variable: the northward wavenumber component of a two-dimensional wavenumber spectrum, defined as `eastward_wave_wavenumber`. |
| `number_of_fitted_spectral_points`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | int `(…)`; `1` | Number of points of the wavenumber-frequency spectrum, i.e. the spectral bins identified as the wave signal, that were used in the least-squares fit of the dispersion relation. |
| `number_of_observations`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | int `(…)`; `1` | Number of Level 1b images that contributed to each grid cell of a relative roughness image or mosaic. |
| `ray_time`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | float `(azimuth)`; `s` | Time of each ray of a polar image cube after the start of its revolution, i.e. the time of the selected Level 1a ray, the correspondingly interpolated time, or the mean time of the averaged rays. It is the same for all images, which assumes that the antenna rotates steadily. |
| `relative_roughness`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | float `(…)`; `1` or `dB` | Relative sea surface roughness: the time mean of the radar backscatter power or amplitude over the averaging window of each grid cell, in uncalibrated analog-to-digital converter units, which is relative because it is not calibrated against a measure of the sea surface roughness. Which of the two is averaged is stated by [`averaged_quantity`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#averaged_quantity): `power`, `amplitude`, or, if the signal cannot be linearized, such as a log-compressed video signal, `stored_values`, the Level 1b values as they are. The mean is stored as that quantity, in linear (`1`) or logarithmic (`dB`) units, and in `1` for `stored_values`. Carries `cell_methods = "time: mean"`, `averaged_quantity`, and `coordinates = "time"`. The averaging window of each grid cell starts at `time`, or at `time + start_time_of_observations` in a mosaic, and lasts at most [`averaging_duration`](../metadata_attributes/processing_parameters.md#averaging_duration); it may be shorter for some cells, as recorded by `number_of_observations`. The optional `time_bounds` give the nominal averaging window of an image, or the measurement period of a mosaic (see [Gridded products](gridded.md#variables)). |
| `scan_sectors`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | byte `(…)`; `0b`, `1b`, `2b` | Flag variable of a relative roughness image mosaic: the part of the radar scan, relative to the target area, that the observations of each grid cell belong to, with the [`flag_meanings`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#flag_meanings) `preceding_target_area` (0), `inside_target_area` (1), and `trailing_target_area` (2). |
| `sea_floor_depth_below_sea_surface`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m` | Water depth, from the sea surface to the sea floor. As the CF standard name implies, it is the depth below the actual sea surface during the measurement period, as sensed by the waves, and therefore includes the tide and other sea level variations; it is not tide-corrected. |
| `sea_floor_depth_below_sea_surface_standard_error`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m` | Standard error of the water depth, e.g. from a least-squares fit. Requires `sea_floor_depth_below_sea_surface`. |
| `sea_ice_x_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the sea ice drift along the x axis of a rotated local grid, from the forward retrieval, defined otherwise as `eastward_sea_ice_velocity` (see [Georeferencing](../georeferencing/index.md#rotated-local-grids)). Takes the place of `eastward_sea_ice_velocity` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_ice_y_velocity`. |
| `sea_ice_x_velocity_backward`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the sea ice drift along the x axis of a rotated local grid, from the backward retrieval, defined otherwise as `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward`. Carries the standard name `sea_ice_x_velocity`. Takes the place of `eastward_sea_ice_velocity_backward` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_ice_y_velocity_backward`. |
| `sea_ice_y_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the sea ice drift along the y axis of a rotated local grid, from the forward retrieval, defined as `sea_ice_x_velocity`. Takes the place of `northward_sea_ice_velocity` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_ice_x_velocity`. |
| `sea_ice_y_velocity_backward`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the sea ice drift along the y axis of a rotated local grid, from the backward retrieval, defined as `sea_ice_x_velocity_backward`. Carries the standard name `sea_ice_y_velocity`. Takes the place of `northward_sea_ice_velocity_backward` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_ice_x_velocity_backward`. |
| `sea_surface_wave_directional_spread`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | float `(…, wave_frequency)`; `degree` | Directional spread of the waves in each frequency band. Requires `wave_frequency`. |
| `sea_surface_wave_directional_variance_spectral_density`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…, wave_frequency, sea_surface_wave_from_direction)`; `m2 s degree-1` | Wave energy density as a function of wave frequency and of the direction from which the waves come. Requires `wave_frequency` and `sea_surface_wave_from_direction`. |
| `sea_surface_wave_from_direction`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(sea_surface_wave_from_direction)` or `(…)`; `degree` | Direction from which the waves come, in degrees clockwise from true north, following the nautical convention for waves. As a coordinate variable, the direction axis of a frequency-direction spectrum. As a data variable, the mean wave direction, whose definition should be documented in a [`comment`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#comment) attribute. |
| `sea_surface_wave_from_direction_at_variance_spectral_density_maximum`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `degree` | Peak wave direction: the direction from which the waves come at the spectral maximum. |
| `sea_surface_wave_mean_from_direction`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | float `(…, wave_frequency)`; `degree` | Mean direction from which the waves come in each frequency band. Requires `wave_frequency`. |
| `sea_surface_wave_mean_period`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `s` | Mean wave period, e.g. $T_{m01}$ or $T_{m02}$; the spectral moment definition should be documented in a [`comment`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#comment) attribute. Where CF defines a standard name for the moment, the variable carries it instead of `sea_surface_wave_mean_period`: `sea_surface_wave_mean_period_from_variance_spectral_density_first_frequency_moment` for $T_{m01}$, `sea_surface_wave_mean_period_from_variance_spectral_density_second_frequency_moment` for $T_{m02}$, and `sea_surface_wave_mean_period_from_variance_spectral_density_inverse_frequency_moment` for $T_{m-10}$. Intrinsic or absolute, as given by [`wave_frequency_frame`](../metadata_attributes/processing_parameters.md#wave_frequency_frame). |
| `sea_surface_wave_mean_square_slope`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Mean square slope of the sea surface, used by the shadowing method to derive the significant wave height. |
| `sea_surface_wave_period_at_variance_spectral_density_maximum`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `s` | Peak wave period: the period at the spectral maximum. Intrinsic or absolute, as given by [`wave_frequency_frame`](../metadata_attributes/processing_parameters.md#wave_frequency_frame). |
| `sea_surface_wave_significant_height`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m` | Significant wave height. |
| `sea_surface_wave_variance_spectral_density`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | float `(…, wave_frequency)`; `m2 s` | Wave energy density as a function of wave frequency. Requires `wave_frequency`. |
| `sea_surface_wave_wavenumber_variance_spectral_density`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…, northward_wave_wavenumber, eastward_wave_wavenumber)` or `(…, y_wave_wavenumber, x_wave_wavenumber)`; `m4` | Calibrated wave energy density as a function of the two wavenumber components. It carries no [`standard_name`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#standard_name): CF defines `sea_surface_wave_variance_spectral_density` for frequency spectra, in units of `m2 s`, to which `m4` cannot be converted, and the variable is named so as not to be mistaken for it. Requires `eastward_wave_wavenumber` and `northward_wave_wavenumber`. |
| `sea_water_x_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the near-surface current along the x axis of a rotated local grid, defined otherwise as `eastward_sea_water_velocity` (see [Georeferencing](../georeferencing/index.md#rotated-local-grids)). Takes the place of `eastward_sea_water_velocity` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_water_y_velocity`. |
| `sea_water_x_velocity_standard_error`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Standard error of the component of the near-surface current along the x axis of a rotated local grid. Takes the place of `eastward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error` on a rotated grid. Requires `sea_water_x_velocity`. Carried together with `sea_water_y_velocity_standard_error`. |
| `sea_water_y_velocity`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Component of the near-surface current along the y axis of a rotated local grid, defined as `sea_water_x_velocity`. Takes the place of `northward_sea_water_velocity` on a rotated grid. Carried together with `sea_water_x_velocity`. |
| `sea_water_y_velocity_standard_error`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `m s-1` | Standard error of the component of the near-surface current along the y axis of a rotated local grid. Takes the place of `northward_sea_water_velocity_standard_error` on a rotated grid. Requires `sea_water_y_velocity`. Carried together with `sea_water_x_velocity_standard_error`. |
| `signal_to_noise_ratio`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Signal-to-noise ratio of the current retrieval, as defined by the method; the definition is given in a [`comment`](../metadata_attributes/variable_attributes.md#comment) attribute. |
| `start_time_of_observations`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | float `(…)`; `s` | Start of the averaging of each grid cell of a relative roughness image mosaic, in seconds after `time`. |
| `wave_frequency`<br><small>[CF][cf-names]</small> | double `(wave_frequency)`; `s-1` | Coordinate variable: wave frequency, intrinsic or absolute, as given by [`wave_frequency_frame`](../metadata_attributes/processing_parameters.md#wave_frequency_frame). |
| `wave_signal`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(…)`; `1` | Spectral density of the wave signal in the radar images, used by the signal-to-noise ratio method to derive the significant wave height. Carried together with `background_noise`. |
| `wavenumber_bin`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(wavenumber_bin)`; `m-1` | Coordinate variable: the center of each wavenumber band of a current profile, cyclic. The current retrieval is repeated in each band, and since the effective sensing depth decreases with increasing wavenumber, the bands resolve the current at different depths. Data variables with this dimension carry it immediately before the dimensions of the horizontal grid. Its `bounds` attribute is `"wavenumber_bin_bounds"`. Requires `wavenumber_bin_bounds`. |
| `wavenumber_bin_bounds`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(wavenumber_bin, nv)`; `m-1` | Lower and upper edge of each wavenumber band, along the dimension `nv` (number of vertices, as in [CF][cf-bounds] section 7.1) of size 2, so that the bands need not be of equal width. Requires `wavenumber_bin`. |
| `x_wave_wavenumber`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(x_wave_wavenumber)`; `m-1` | Coordinate variable: the wavenumber component along the x axis of a rotated local grid of a two-dimensional wavenumber spectrum, defined otherwise as `eastward_wave_wavenumber` (see [Georeferencing](../georeferencing/index.md#rotated-local-grids)). Takes the place of `eastward_wave_wavenumber` on a rotated grid. |
| `y_wave_wavenumber`<br><small>SOMaR</small> | double `(y_wave_wavenumber)`; `m-1` | Coordinate variable: the wavenumber component along the y axis of a rotated local grid of a two-dimensional wavenumber spectrum, defined as `x_wave_wavenumber`. Takes the place of `northward_wave_wavenumber` on a rotated grid. |
